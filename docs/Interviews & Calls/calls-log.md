Every call you place to a candidate is captured in one place — with its recording, transcript, and AI summary — so you never have to reconstruct a conversation from memory. The **Calls** page is your workspace-wide call history; this guide shows you how to read it, search it, and dig into a single call.

## 1. Open the Calls page

Click **Calls** in the sidebar. At the top you'll see the page title with a running count of calls, plus four quick stats:

- **Total calls**
- **Completed**
- **Avg duration**
- **Answer rate** — the share of your calls that were answered and completed

{/* 📸 Screenshot: The Calls page with the stats strip and a few days of call history */}

Below the stats, calls are grouped by day — **Today**, **Yesterday**, then recent weekdays and dates — newest first. Each row shows the candidate, the pipeline they belong to, who called which number, the time, the call duration, and a status dot.

<Callout icon="💡" theme="info">
  The Calls page lists the calls you and your teammates place directly to candidates — for example with the **Call** button on a [candidate profile](/docs/candidate-profiles). Calls that happen inside a pipeline (AI voice interviews, manual phone screens) live on the candidate's profile under their **Calls** tab. See [AI Voice Interviews](/docs/ai-voice-interviews).
</Callout>

## 2. Find a call

Use the search box at the top — **Search by candidate, number, pipeline…** — to filter the list. It matches candidate names, phone numbers, pipeline names, and the caller's name all at once, so a single search like a candidate's first name or a pipeline title is usually enough.

Call statuses tell you what happened at a glance:

- **Completed** — the call connected and finished
- **No answer**, **Busy**, **Canceled**, **Failed** — the call didn't happen
- **Initiated**, **Ringing**, **In progress** — the call is happening right now

## 3. Review a call

Click any row to open the call detail panel. The header shows the candidate, their pipeline, the status, and the date, time, and duration — with a link icon to jump straight to the candidate's full profile.

{/* 📸 Screenshot: The call detail panel open on a completed call, showing the audio player and Summary tab */}

If the call was recorded, an audio player sits at the top — press play, or click anywhere on the progress bar to jump around the recording. Below it, three tabs break down the conversation:

- **Summary** — an AI-written recap of the call, ready to paste into notes or share with the team.
- **Transcript** — the full text of the conversation, with a **Copy** button.
- **Details** — candidate, recipient number, pipeline, caller, date, duration, and status, plus a score out of 100 for pipeline interview calls.

Right after a call ends you may briefly see **Transcribing…** while the recording is processed — the summary and transcript appear automatically once it finishes.

## 4. Actions on a call

From the **Details** tab you can:

- **Download recording** — save the audio file.
- **Re-process** — re-run transcription and the AI summary for the recording. Handy if you see **Transcription failed**.
- **Delete call** — remove the call permanently. You'll be asked to click again to confirm.

<Callout icon="⚠️" theme="warn">
  Deleting a call removes its recording, transcript, and summary for the whole workspace — there's no undo. Some actions, like deleting calls or seeing the Calls page at all, depend on your role; if you see "You don't have access to calls", ask a workspace admin. See [Team Members & Roles](/docs/team-members-roles).
</Callout>
