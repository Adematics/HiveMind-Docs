Phone-screen candidates without leaving Hivemind, and without a phone system. You call straight from the candidate's profile; the call is recorded and transcribed automatically, and both attach to the candidate's record the moment you hang up. Three weeks later you can pull up exactly what was said, and your hiring manager reads the actual conversation instead of a second-hand summary.

## 1. Place a call

Open any candidate and hit **Call**. There's nothing to set up: no telephony contract, no separate dialer, no per-seat phone line.

{/* 📸 Screenshot: a candidate profile with the Call button and the in-call state */}

## 2. The recording and transcript file themselves

When the call ends, the recording and the transcript attach to the candidate's record automatically. Past calls are searchable by candidate name, and every call also appears in the workspace-wide [Calls Log](/docs/calls-log) with its recording, transcript, and AI summary.

{/* 📸 Screenshot: a completed call on the candidate record showing the recording player and transcript */}

## 3. Billing

Calls are billed from your existing credit balance by duration. Current rates are on the [pricing page](https://hivemind.hr/pricing); see [Credits & Billing](/docs/credits-billing) for how the balance works.

<Callout icon="💡" theme="info">
  Want the first screen to happen without a human on the line at all? An AI agent can place the call, screen against your criteria, and file the transcript the same way. See [AI Voice Interviews](/docs/ai-voice-interviews).
</Callout>
