In Hivemind, a job and its hiring pipeline are the same thing: you describe the role, choose the steps applicants go through, and activate it. From that moment the job is live on your careers page, has a shareable apply link, and every applicant flows into the pipeline automatically. This guide walks you through it end to end in about fifteen minutes.

<Callout icon="💡" theme="info">
  Before you start, connect a mailbox under **Settings → Email Services** so Hivemind can email applicants, and publish your [company page](/docs/your-company-page) if you want the job listed on your careers site.
</Callout>

## 1. Describe the job

1. Go to **My Pipelines** (**View all** under **Inbound** in the sidebar) and click **New Pipeline**.
2. Pick the **Manual** tab.
3. Fill in the job:
   - **Job Title** (required), and optionally a **Seniority**, which is added to the front of the title (*Senior Backend Engineer*).
   - **Pipeline name** (required): your internal name, e.g. *Backend Hiring Q3*.
   - **Workplace Type**, **Location** (a **Region** for Remote roles, otherwise **City** and **Country**), and **Job Type**.
4. Next to **Description**, click **Draft with AI**, add any notes you want reflected (team, must-have tools, salary range), and click **Generate**. Edit the draft in place until it reads the way you want.
5. Click **Preview posting** to see the job exactly as a candidate will read it.
6. Click **Create Pipeline**.

{/* 📸 Screenshot: the Manual create form with the job fields filled in and the Draft with AI panel open */}

Prefer a guided setup? The **Hive** tab builds the job description, steps, and assessment from a short chat, and creates the pipeline already active. Details for both routes are in [Creating a Pipeline](/docs/creating-a-pipeline).

## 2. Choose the hiring steps

You land in the pipeline editor with a **Start** card and the **Application Form** already in place. Drag steps from the palette on the left onto a **Drag a step here** slot, for example **Resume Screening**, then a **Skills Assessment**, a **Candidate Review**, and a **Video Interview**. Click each step to configure it, then click **Save** in the header.

To change what applicants fill in, click the **Application Form** step and edit its fields.

See the [Pipeline Nodes Reference](/docs/pipeline-nodes-reference) for every step and its settings.

## 3. Activate it

Click **Activate** in the pipeline header. Hivemind checks two things first:

- **Every step is configured.** If not, you'll see how many steps need attention, and they're marked in red on the canvas.
- **The job details are complete**: title, description, location, workplace type, and job type. If anything is missing you'll see **Incomplete Pipeline** and the **Settings** dialog opens so you can fill it in.

When it passes, you'll see **Pipeline Activated**: *"Your pipeline is now live and accepting applications."*

<Callout icon="⚠️" theme="warn">
  An active pipeline runs for real: applicants get your emails, assessments, and interview invitations from the first minute. Review each step before you activate.
</Callout>

## 4. Where your job appears

Once active, the job shows up in three places:

- **Your careers page.** The **Open Positions** section of your [company page](/docs/your-company-page) lists every active pipeline. Each role has its own job page with **About this role** (your description), **What to expect** (the steps applicants will go through), an **Apply now** button, **Remind me later**, and **Copy link to this job**.
- **The application page.** **Apply now** leads to a form-first page: your logo, the job title and key facts, then the form, so candidates can apply from the first screen. A **View job details** link takes them back to the full job page.
- **Anywhere you share the link.** Copy the apply link with **Application Link** in the pipeline's **Manage** menu or the row actions on the **My Pipelines** table, the **Application link** row on the Start card, or the share icon next to the pipeline's name. Post it on job boards, social, or in an email.

See [The Candidate Experience](/docs/the-candidate-experience) for what applicants see after they apply.

## 5. Post it to LinkedIn (optional)

With a LinkedIn account connected, Hivemind offers to share the job as soon as you activate it, and **Share to LinkedIn** stays in the pipeline's **Manage** menu for later. Applicants from LinkedIn click through to your Hivemind application form and land in the same pipeline. Setup and options are in [LinkedIn Job Posting](/docs/linkedin-job-posting).

## 6. Watch applicants arrive

Open the pipeline's **Manage** menu and choose **Applicants** to see everyone who has applied, the step they're on, and their scores. Steps that need your decision, such as **Candidate Review**, show up in your **Inbox**. You can also add a referral or someone who emailed you directly with **Add candidate**, at whichever stage you choose.

Everything about working with applicants is in [Managing Candidates in a Pipeline](/docs/managing-candidates-in-a-pipeline).
