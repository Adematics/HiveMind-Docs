Advertise a Hivemind job on LinkedIn without retyping it. You can publish it as a job on your LinkedIn Company Page or share it as a post on your feed. Either way, applicants click through to your Hivemind application page and land in the pipeline you already built, with your screening, assessments and automations running from the first minute.

## 1. Connect your LinkedIn account

Go to **Settings → Apps & Integrations**, click the **LinkedIn** tile and connect your account. This is a one-time step. Sharing to LinkedIn is offered once you have a connected LinkedIn account that has finished syncing.

{/* 📸 Screenshot: Settings → Apps & Integrations with the LinkedIn tile connected */}

## 2. Open Share to LinkedIn

You can share any **active** pipeline:

- **When you activate a pipeline**, Hivemind offers the **Share to LinkedIn** dialog straight away. Click **Not now** to skip it.
- **Any time later**, choose **Share to LinkedIn** from the pipeline's **Manage** menu on the pipeline page, or from the pipeline's menu in the sidebar. It isn't in the row actions on the pipelines table.

If you have more than one LinkedIn account connected, pick one under **LinkedIn account**.

## 3. Publish a job on your Company Page

If you administer a LinkedIn Company Page, the dialog opens on **Posting details**:

1. Choose the **Company Page** to post under.
2. Check the **Job title** and **Job description**. Both start from the pipeline and can be edited here.
3. **Location**, **Workplace** and **Employment type** come from the pipeline's settings.
4. Click **Publish job**.

You'll see **Job posted to LinkedIn** with a **View on LinkedIn** link. If LinkedIn accepts the job but Hivemind can't publish it automatically, you'll see **Draft created** instead: click **Open on LinkedIn** to review and publish it there.

From the result screen, **Share as a post** lets you announce the job on your feed as well.

{/* 📸 Screenshot: the Share to LinkedIn dialog showing Posting details and the Publish job button */}

## 4. Or share it as a feed post

If you don't administer a Company Page, the dialog goes straight to **Feed post**:

1. Under **Post as**, choose a Company Page or **Your personal profile** (only shown when you have a Company Page to choose from).
2. Edit the **Post**. Hivemind drafts it for you with the job title and your application link, so LinkedIn shows a preview card.
3. Click **Post to LinkedIn**.

## 5. Applicants land in your pipeline

Whichever way you share, candidates apply through your Hivemind application page and enter the pipeline automatically, exactly like an applicant who came from your [application link](/docs/pipeline-nodes-reference) or [company page](/docs/your-company-page). Your first pipeline steps (Resume Scoring, assessments, communication) run without any manual routing.

<Callout icon="💡" theme="info">
  The job you maintain in Hivemind is the job candidates see. If it changes, update the pipeline and share it again rather than maintaining two diverging versions.
</Callout>
