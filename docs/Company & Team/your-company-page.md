Your company page is the public careers site candidates see: your story, your culture, and every open role, all pulled straight from your pipelines. You build it with a drag-and-drop editor: open **Settings → Career Page** to get started.

The builder lives in Settings alongside the rest of your company configuration. Your company name, logo, and cover image come from **Settings → Workspace Settings**, so you set them once and the page, the candidate portal, and the rest of Hivemind all use them.

<Callout icon="💡" theme="info">
  The published page looks great on any device, and the builder's **Preview** lets you check it as **Desktop**, **Tablet**, or **Mobile** before you publish.
</Callout>

## 1. Pick a starting point

The first time you open the builder you'll be asked to **Choose a template** (**Modern**, **Classic**, or **Bold**), each pre-filled with sensible sections and copy you can rewrite. Prefer a blank canvas? Click **Start from scratch** and you'll begin with just a **Hero Banner** and **Open Positions**.

{/* 📸 Screenshot: the Choose a Template screen showing the Modern, Classic, and Bold template cards */}

## 2. Add and arrange sections

Your page's sections are listed in the **Sections** panel on the left, with a preview of the page beside it. Click **Add section** (or the **+** between two sections to add one right there) and pick from:

- **Hero Banner**: headline, subheadline, background image, and a call-to-action button
- **About Us**: your story, founding year, size, industry, and location
- **Team Members**: photos and roles
- **Open Positions**: live job listings from your pipelines
- **Company Culture**, **Benefits & Perks**, and **Testimonials**
- **Media Gallery** and **Custom Content**, the only two you can add more than once

Click a section to edit its content, drag it (or use **Move up** and **Move down**) to reorder, hide or show it, or remove it. Hidden sections never appear on the public page. Undo and redo are always a click (or **Ctrl+Z** and **Ctrl+Shift+Z**) away.

The builder doesn't auto-save: nothing reaches your workspace until you click **Save** in the toolbar, so an image you're only trying out never commits itself. The status bar shows **Unsaved changes** until you do, then **All changes saved**. If you navigate away before saving, your unsaved work stays on that device, and next time you open the builder a notice offers to **Restore** it or **Discard** it.

{/* 📸 Screenshot: the builder canvas with the Sections panel on the left and a section's config panel open on the right */}

## 3. How your jobs appear

The **Open Positions** section lists your **active** pipelines automatically: activate a pipeline and it shows up; close one and it disappears. Prefer to curate? Turn off auto-pull in the section's settings and hand-pick which pipelines to show. Draft pipelines never appear. See [Creating a Pipeline](/docs/creating-a-pipeline) for how activation works.

Each listing opens that role's own job page on your career site, branded like the rest of your page. It shows the job title with its location, workplace type, and job type, the job description under **About this role**, and a **What to expect** outline of the steps the candidate will go through (for example Application, Resume review, Skills assessment, Interview). Candidates can click **Apply now** to go straight to the application form, **Remind me later** to get an email with a link back to the role, or **Copy link to this job** to share it. Once a pipeline closes, its job page says **No longer accepting applications** and points visitors to your other open roles.

## 4. Set your branding and URL

Your **company name**, **logo**, and **cover image** are set in **Settings → Workspace Settings**, not in the builder. Changes there show up in the builder straight away. That's also where you'll find **Show cover on candidate portal**, which puts your cover at the top of every candidate's portal page as soon as you save, with no need to publish the career page.

For everything specific to the page, click the **Page settings** icon in the toolbar. It has three tabs:

- **Appearance**: the page's **Accent Color**, used for buttons, links, and highlights (pick a preset or enter any hex colour), and the **Visitor Theme**, which sets how the page looks when someone first opens it: **Light**, **Dark**, or **Automatic**.
- **SEO**: the **Page Title**, **Meta Description**, and social **Share Image** used when your page appears in search results and link previews.
- **Publishing**: your **Page URL** (lowercase letters, numbers, and hyphens only); click **Update URL** to save it. Links you've already shared with the old address stop working. This tab is also where you can **Unpublish page** to take it off the web while keeping your draft, sections, and address.

## 5. Publish and share

1. Use the **Preview** button anytime to see the page exactly as candidates will.
2. When you're happy, click **Publish**. Publishing saves your latest changes and makes them live. If a section is missing required content (say, a hero without a headline), you'll get a **Cannot publish yet** message and the builder jumps to the section that needs attention.
3. Once published, the toolbar shows a **Live** badge, and the **View live page** icon opens the real page in a new tab. If you edit the page afterwards, the badge notes **Unpublished changes** until you publish again.

To share it, open the page with **View live page** and copy the address, or use **Settings → Workspace Settings**, where the **Public Company Page** card has a one-click **Copy link** button.

<Callout icon="⚠️" theme="warn">
  Your public page only shows the design you built after you **Publish**. Before that, visitors see a simple default layout, so if your edits aren't showing up live, hit **Publish** first, then refresh.
</Callout>
