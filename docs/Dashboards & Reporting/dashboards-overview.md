Dashboards turn everything already in your account into boards you can read at a glance: how each pipeline is converting, which candidates are moving, how interviews and assessments are going, and where your credits went. Open **Dashboards** in the sidebar to get there.

There are two kinds of board, and the difference matters:

- **Core boards** are built in and always current. Hivemind hydrates them live every time you open one, so there is nothing to set up and nothing to keep up to date.
- **Saved boards** are yours. You build them, name them, arrange the cards, and come back to them.

{/* 📸 Screenshot: the Dashboards index showing the core-board rail and saved boards */}

## 1. Start with a core board

Six core boards ship with every account, listed at the top of the left rail:

| Board | What it answers |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | How is hiring going overall right now? |
| **Pipelines** | Where are candidates converting, and where are they stalling? |
| **Candidates** | Who is in play, and how did they get there? |
| **Interviews** | What is scheduled, held, and still waiting on a review? |
| **Assessments** | How are candidates performing on what you send them? |
| **Credits** | Where is the balance going? |

Core boards are read-only as they stand. Change anything on one and Hivemind forks your version into a saved board rather than overwriting the built-in, so you can always get back to the original. Use **Save a copy** to do that deliberately.

## 2. Filter what you are looking at

Every board has two controls at the top: a searchable **pipeline picker** and a **time range**. Pick a pipeline and the whole board narrows to it; change the range and every card re-reads against the new window. Board and card titles follow the range, so a board never claims to show thirty days while displaying ninety.

A **refresh** control re-pulls the data without dropping the filters you have set.

## 3. Build your own board

From **Dashboards**, create a board and you land in the editor. Cards sit on a twelve-column grid:

- Drag a card to move it, and drag its corner to resize in either direction.
- Cards snap to whole columns and pack together, so a board cannot end up with holes in it.
- KPI cards compress to compact chips, so a row of numbers stays a row of numbers.

Boards you are still working on stay as **drafts** until you publish them; the index lists drafts and published boards separately, with a summary of the widgets on each.

Press the fullscreen control to fill the screen with a single board, and **Esc** to come back.

{/* 📸 Screenshot: the board editor with a card mid-resize on the twelve-column grid */}

## 4. Ask Hive instead of building by hand

Hive is the chat dock that sits alongside your boards. Describe the board you want and it builds it; ask for a change while a board is open and the change lands on that board in place, with no overlay to dismiss and no copy to reconcile.

Openers that work well:

- *Give me a hiring overview for the last 30 days*
- *Compare my pipelines by applications and scores*
- *Who moved stages this week?*

Hive knows which board you are looking at, so follow-ups can be short: *now split that by pipeline*, *make it the last quarter instead*.

<Callout icon="💡" theme="info">
  Hive is not only for dashboards. The same box answers questions about candidates and builds pipelines for you, so *create a pipeline for a senior backend engineer* works from here too.
</Callout>

<Callout icon="⚠️" theme="warn">
  A request that is not about the board you have open forks a new draft instead of overwriting your work. If a board you expected to change did not, look for a new draft on the Dashboards index.
</Callout>
