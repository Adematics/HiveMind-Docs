Not every group of candidates maps to a pipeline. A shortlist for the hiring manager, people to revisit next quarter, the strongest applicants across three roles: **Candidate Lists** give those groups a home in Hivemind, so they don't live in a spreadsheet or someone's head.

A list only organizes. Adding someone to a list doesn't move them in any pipeline, removing them only takes them off the list, and deleting a list never deletes the candidates on it.

## 1. Create a list

1. Click **Candidate Lists** in the sidebar.
2. Click **New List**.
3. Give it a **Title** (required) and, if you like, a **Description** of what it's for.
4. Click **Create list**.

The **Candidate Lists** page shows every list with its **Title**, **Description**, number of **Candidates**, and when it was **Created** and **Updated**. Use the search box to find a list by title or description, and click a list to open it.

{/* 📸 Screenshot: the Candidate Lists page with several lists and the New List button */}

You can also create a list on the fly: the **Add to list** dialog has a **New list** button, so you never have to leave what you're doing.

## 2. Add candidates

There are three ways in.

**From a candidates table.** On the **Candidates** page or any pipeline's applicants table, select a row (or right-click it) and choose **Add to list**. To add several at once, tick their rows and choose **Add to list** from the bulk actions. Pick a list, then click **Add**.

**From a candidate's profile.** Open the **Manage** menu in the profile header and choose **Add to list**.

**From inside the list.** Open the list and click **Add candidates**. Search or filter to narrow the candidates, then either:

- tick the people you want and click **Add** (the button shows how many are selected), or
- click **Add all … matching** to add everyone your search and filters match, up to **2,000** at a time. If more than 2,000 match, narrow your filters first.

Candidates already on the list aren't shown in the picker and are skipped if you add them again from a table, so no one is added twice.

{/* 📸 Screenshot: the Add candidates dialog inside a list, with filters applied and the Add all matching button */}

<Callout icon="💡" theme="info">
  Ticked rows in the **Add candidates** dialog belong to the page you're on. If you change page or filters, the ticks clear, so add each page's picks before moving on, or use **Add all … matching**.
</Callout>

## 3. Work with a list

An open list shows its candidates in the same table you use everywhere else, with the same search, filters, columns, and row actions, so you can email a shortlist, export it, or open any profile.

- **Remove from list**: select one or more candidates and choose **Remove from list**. They stay in their pipelines.
- **Edit**: rename the list or update its description.
- **Delete**: on the **Candidate Lists** page, select a list (or several) and choose **Delete** or **Delete Selected**. Only the grouping is removed; the candidates stay in their pipelines.

## Lists and your AI assistant

If you've connected an AI assistant to Hivemind, it can work with your lists too: compare candidates and save a shortlist into **Candidate Lists**, read who is on a list, or rank a list's candidates. It always asks you before saving a shortlist, removing people from a list, or deleting one. See [Hivemind MCP Server](/docs/hivemind-mcp-server).

<Callout icon="⚠️" theme="warn">
  Whether you can create lists, add to them, or remove people depends on your role. If you don't see **Add to list** or **New List**, ask a workspace admin. See [Team Members & Roles](/docs/team-members-roles).
</Callout>
