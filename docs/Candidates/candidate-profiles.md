Every applicant in your workspace gets a single profile that follows them across pipelines: one place to see their resume, responses, scores, interview recordings, offers, and every email you've exchanged. This guide covers the global **Candidates** page and the full profile view.

## The Candidates list

Open **Candidates** from the sidebar to see everyone across all your pipelines. To narrow the list:

- **Quick filters**: one-click chips for **Completed**, **Ongoing**, **Attention** (candidates stuck waiting on credits or setup), **Assessment**, and **Hired**, each with a live count.
- **Search**: the search box matches candidates as you type.
- **Filter** and **Sort**: build precise filters on any column and order the results; **Clear all** resets everything.
- **View**: show or hide columns such as **Skills**, **Referral Tag**, or **Hired**.

The table shows columns like **Name**, **Email**, **Phone**, **Score**, **Status**, **Current Node**, and **Pipeline**. Click **View Resume** in the **Resume** column to open a candidate's resume right in the app, or click the **Pipeline** value to jump to that pipeline's applicants table.

{/* 📸 Screenshot: The Candidates list with quick-filter chips, search box, and the Filter / Sort / View / Export controls visible */}

Need the data elsewhere? Click **Export** to download the current view (rows, columns, and filters included) as a CSV.

<Callout icon="💡" theme="info">
  Select one or more rows to act in bulk: **Send Email** sends a message to everyone selected, and **Delete** removes them. Deleting is permanent: it erases all responses, notifications, and history.
</Callout>

## Quick view and full profile

Clicking a row opens a quick-view panel with the essentials: score, contact details, **Highlights**, **Application Details**, recent **Responses**, **Comments**, and **Tasks**. Use the arrows to flip through candidates without leaving the list, or click **Full Profile** to open the complete record.

The full profile organizes everything into tabs:

- **Overview**: highlights, application details, and anything **Awaiting your review**.
- **Pipelines**: one card per application, with a step-by-step journey timeline showing each stage the candidate passed, its score, and expandable answers. Buttons open their **Resume**, original **Application**, and full **Report**.
- **Assessments**: every assessment taken, grouped by pipeline, with scores and expandable per-question reports. See [Reviewing Results](/docs/reviewing-results).
- **Offers**: offer letters and their signing status. See [Offers](/docs/offers).
- **Outreach**, **Tasks**, **Emails**, **Calls**, and **SMS**: campaign history, to-dos, and every conversation.

A side panel adds an **Activity** feed (a timestamped timeline of events like "Applied to Pipeline", "Assessment Completed", "Score Updated", and "Marked as Hired") plus **Emails** and team **Comments**.

## Scores, transcripts, and recordings

Scores run 0–100 and are color-coded: green for 80 and up, amber for 50–79, red below. For voice interviews, the profile shows the **Recording** with a built-in player, the full **Transcript**, an AI-written **Summary**, and an **Evaluation**. See [AI Voice Interviews](/docs/ai-voice-interviews).

## Managing a candidate

The **Manage** menu (gear icon) is where actions live:

- **Send email**: compose directly from the profile.
- **Share report link** and **Share portal link**: copy links to the recruiter report or the candidate's own portal.
- **Edit score**: override the overall score (0–100).
- **Change stage**: opens **Manage Progress**, where you can **Rewind** a candidate to an earlier step or **Skip Ahead**.
- **Mark as hired** / **Reset hiring status**.
- **Remove from pipeline**: deletes that one application; other pipelines are untouched.
- **Delete from all pipelines**: erases the candidate everywhere.

<Callout icon="⚠️" theme="warn">
  **Rewind** permanently deletes the responses from every step after the one you rewind to; the candidate will redo those assessments. Removals and deletions can't be undone either, so double-check before confirming.
</Callout>
