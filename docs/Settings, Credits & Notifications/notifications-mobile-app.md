Hivemind keeps you posted through three channels: the **Inbox** inside the app, email summaries, and push notifications on the Hivemind mobile app. They all draw from the same stream of tasks (things that need your attention, like a candidate waiting on manual review or a reply landing in a conversation), so wherever you see the alert, acting on it clears it everywhere.

## Your Inbox: notifications as tasks

In Hivemind, a notification *is* a task. Click **Inbox** in the sidebar; the red badge shows how many unread items are waiting for you. The Inbox is a two-pane view: a list of tasks on the left, and the selected task's details on the right, where you can act on it directly with buttons like **Approve**, **Disapprove**, **Skip**, **Schedule Interview**, or **Mark as Resolved**.

{/* 📸 Screenshot: Inbox page with the task list on the left, a Candidate Review task open on the right, and the red unread badge visible on the sidebar */}

Opening a task marks it as read automatically. Resolving it (or completing the action it asks for) marks it done and removes it from your open count.

<Callout icon="💡" theme="info">
  The badge only counts what you're allowed to see. For example, review and scheduling tasks appear only for roles that can perform manual reviews, and message-reply tasks only for roles with email access. See [Team Members & Roles](/docs/team-members-roles).
</Callout>

## What triggers a notification

- **Candidate Review**: a candidate reaches a manual review stage, a video interview recording is ready for review, or a phone call recording needs review
- **Schedule Interview**: an interview or phone call needs a time picked, or a candidate submitted their availability
- **Phone call reminders**: a scheduled phone call is about to start
- **Message Reply**: a candidate replies in a conversation
- **Mention Comment**: a teammate @mentions you in a comment on a candidate
- **New Email**: an inbound email arrives from a candidate, or one of your emails bounces
- **Offer updates**: a candidate signs, declines, or requests changes to an offer, or an offer is withdrawn (see [Offers](/docs/offers))

## Managing your Inbox

Click **Filters** to slice the list: a **Status** toggle (**Open**, **Done**, **All**), a **Read** toggle (**All**, **Unread**, **Read**), a **Date range**, and type chips: **Candidate Review**, **Message Reply**, **Mention Comment**, **General Task**, **Schedule Interview**, and **New Email**. You can also narrow the list to a single pipeline with the **All pipelines** selector.

{/* 📸 Screenshot: The Filters popover open, showing Status, Read, Date range, and the task type chips */}

## Email notifications

Two kinds of email keep you covered when you're not in the app:

- **Mention emails**: when a teammate @mentions you in a candidate comment, you get an email right away.
- **Notification Summary**: twice a week (Monday and Thursday mornings), every workspace member receives a summary email with the number of open tasks and a link back to the Inbox.

<Callout icon="⚠️" theme="warn">
  The summary email goes out as long as your workspace has open tasks. If you'd rather not hear about the same items twice a week, work through your Inbox and resolve them; an empty Inbox means no summary.
</Callout>

## The Hivemind mobile app

Install the Hivemind mobile app and sign in with your usual account. That's the whole setup. Signing in registers your device for push notifications automatically; no pairing codes needed. You'll get pushes for mentions, candidate replies, review tasks, scheduling tasks, and new inbound emails, and tapping one takes you straight to that task in the app.

Signing out of the mobile app unregisters the device, so pushes stop, which is handy on shared devices.
