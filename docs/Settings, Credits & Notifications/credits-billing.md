Credits are Hivemind's single currency for everything the platform does on your behalf — AI resume screening, assessment evaluation, voice interviews, outreach messages, and candidate sourcing. Instead of juggling separate meters, one balance powers it all. Manage everything from **Settings → Credits**, where you'll see your **Current Credits** balance, your subscription, and three tabs: **Subscription Plans**, **Buy Credits**, and **Transaction History**.

{/* 📸 Screenshot: Credits tab showing the Current Credits card, Current Subscription card, and the three tabs */}

Every new account starts on the free plan with **3,000 credits**, enough to run candidates through a full pipeline and try every feature before paying.

## What consumes credits

Credits are deducted automatically as candidates move through your pipelines and as you use AI features:

- **AI resume screening** — 10 credits per candidate
- **Assessment evaluation** — 300 credits per candidate
- **AI voice interview** — 1,500 credits for a 10-minute call, adjusted at 150 credits per minute (shorter calls are partially refunded, longer calls cost more)
- **Personality test** — 80 credits per candidate
- **IQ test** — 80 credits per candidate
- **Pipeline emails** — 1 credit each; **SMS** — 100 credits each
- **Outreach campaign steps** — 1 credit per email, 3 per WhatsApp message, 5 per SMS, LinkedIn invite, or LinkedIn message, and 10 per InMail
- **People Search** — 30 credits per profile returned (you're only charged for results actually found)

<Callout icon="💡" theme="info">
  If you connect your own OpenAI key under **Settings → Apps & Integrations**, AI evaluation stages (like resume screening) run on your key and skip the credit charge. Calls, emails, SMS, and tests are always billed in credits.
</Callout>

## Subscription plans

The **Subscription Plans** tab shows two monthly plans that refill your balance automatically:

- **Starter Org** — $99/month, includes 10,000 credits. For small teams hiring for one role.
- **Mid Sized Org** — $499/month, includes 65,000 credits. For scaling companies.

Click **Subscribe Now** and you'll be taken to a secure Stripe checkout to pay by card. Need more? Click **Schedule a Demo** to talk about the Enterprise plan for high-volume recruiting.

### Switching plans

Already subscribed? Click **Switch to** the other plan. The change doesn't happen immediately — a **Plan Change Scheduled** notice confirms your plan switches on your next billing date, so you keep everything you've paid for until then.

## Buying credits directly

No subscription required — the **Buy Credits** tab sells one-off top-ups at $10 per 1,000 credits. Pick a package (1,000 up to 50,000 credits) and click **Buy Now**, or enter a **Custom Amount** (minimum 1,000, in steps of 1,000). Either way you'll complete payment through Stripe checkout, and the credits land in your balance right after.

## Transaction history

The **Transaction History** tab is your audit trail: every purchase, subscription refill, usage charge, and refund, with **Amount**, **Type**, **Date**, and **Status** columns. Click a row to expand the full details — handy for seeing exactly which pipeline stage or search consumed credits.

## When credits run out

Hivemind never charges you into the negative — actions that need credits simply pause:

- A candidate about to enter a paid stage stays at their current stage; the move is rolled back and no charge is made. Top up and move them forward again from the pipeline.
- Voice calls won't start, outreach campaigns won't launch, and People Search is blocked until you add credits.

<Callout icon="⚠️" theme="warn">
  Running dry mid-hire can stall active candidates without an obvious error on their side. Keep an eye on the **Current Credits** card — only roles with billing permission can see this tab, so make sure someone on the team owns it.
</Callout>
