Something not working, or just want a human to walk you through Hivemind? Here's every way to reach us, fastest first.

## Chat with us in the app

The quickest route: click **Chat with Us** at the bottom of the sidebar, next to **Settings**. It opens a live support chat right inside Hivemind — no ticket forms, no leaving the page. Because you're signed in, the team already knows which workspace you're writing from, so you can skip the preamble and get straight to the problem.

{/* 📸 Screenshot: the sidebar footer with the Chat with Us item highlighted, and the support chat window open */}

<Callout icon="💡" theme="info">
  Candidates get help too — the same chat bubble is available on application and assessment pages, so applicants who hit a snag mid-assessment can reach support without involving you.
</Callout>

## Book a call

Prefer to talk it through? Use the [**Book a Call**](https://hivemind.hr/book-a-call) page on the Hivemind website — the **Book a Guided Demo** buttons across the site (including on the signup screen) take you straight there. Pick a time that suits you and a member of the team will give you a guided tour, help plan your first pipelines, or dig into anything specific to your workspace. The [**Contact Us**](https://hivemind.hr/contact-us) page on the website offers the same scheduler in a compact form.

{/* 📸 Screenshot: the Book a Call page with the scheduling widget */}

## Email us

For anything that isn't urgent — billing questions, feedback, partnership inquiries — email [**support@gethivemind.ai**](mailto:support@gethivemind.ai) (also linked in the website footer under **Email**). Include your workspace's company name so we can find your account quickly.

## Before you reach out

A surprising number of issues have one-line fixes — a pipeline still in draft, a mailbox that was never connected, a role missing a permission. Skim the [FAQ / Common Issues](/docs/faq-common-issues) first; it may save you the wait entirely.

<Callout icon="⚠️" theme="warn">
  When reporting a problem, mention the pipeline or candidate it involves and roughly when it happened. It's the difference between a same-message answer and a round of follow-up questions.
</Callout>
